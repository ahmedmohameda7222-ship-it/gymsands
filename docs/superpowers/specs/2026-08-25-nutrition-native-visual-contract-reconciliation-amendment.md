# Nutrition Native Visual Contract — Reconciliation Amendment

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Status:** User-approved binding amendment  
**Applies to:** `2026-08-23-nutrition-native-visual-contract-design.md`  
**Parent authority:** `2026-08-25-nutrition-wide-reconciliation-design.md`

This amendment changes only shared-scope and touch-target wording. All other approved visual-system rules remain governed by the original Native Visual Contract.

## 1. Shared scope

The Native Visual Contract now governs four peer Nutrition destinations only:

- Diary;
- Meal Plan;
- Food Library;
- My Recipes.

`Summary` is removed from Nutrition visual scope. Future Global Summary requires its own cross-domain product/visual design and may reuse compatible tokens only through that future design.

## 2. Page-title/composition cleanup

Any original page-title, page-composition, responsive, roadmap, or acceptance wording that lists `Summary` as a Nutrition sibling is stale and superseded.

The four Nutrition pages continue to share the approved native system-led restraint, semantic data palette, typography, materials, state language, RTL, Dynamic Type, and accessibility rules.

## 3. Touch-target wording

Canonical product wording:

> **Plaivra uses ≥44×44 pt effective touch targets as its product baseline on Apple touch surfaces, with larger task-specific controls where appropriate. System-owned controls retain their native geometry and behavior.**

Do not describe 44×44 pt as an Apple absolute minimum applying identically to every system-owned control.

Android continues to use an approximately 48 dp product/platform-aligned interactive baseline where appropriate.

Cooking Mode retains its approved larger page-specific controls, including the approximately 56 pt visible primary Done control and other task-specific minimums defined by My Recipes.

## 4. Summary semantic data palette

Original wording saying `Diary and Summary` must share a semantic data palette is narrowed to the active Nutrition program: Diary and any Nutrition surfaces that visualize the same nutrition facts use the same semantic nutrition mapping. A future Global Summary may adopt that mapping for its Nutrition tab only through its own approved design.

## 5. Authority resolution

This amendment supersedes original Native Visual Contract wording only where it:

- says there are five canonical Nutrition destinations;
- lists `Summary` as a Nutrition page/title/composition;
- makes Summary design a gate to Nutrition completion;
- states the Apple 44×44 target as an absolute platform minimum rather than Plaivra's product baseline.

All other visual-system clauses remain intact.
